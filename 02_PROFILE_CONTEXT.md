# 02 — Profile Context

## Engineering direction

Raushan Kumar builds product-facing systems around AI, software architecture, and dependable workflows. The work emphasizes explicit boundaries between signals, models, decisions, interfaces, and operations.

## What to emphasize

- Intelligent systems that make uncertainty and risk legible.
- Product engineering that begins with a focused workflow and grows from evidence.
- Modular boundaries, configuration, validation, and maintainable interfaces.
- A learning practice based on observation, falsification, model selection, and iteration.

## Writing tone

Calm, precise, evidence-led, and technically literate. Describe systems in terms of outcomes, boundaries, trade-offs, and operating behavior. Prefer “builds intelligent systems” over technology inventories. Do not use student-showcase language, unverified impact claims, decorative hype, skill ratings, badge walls, greetings, quotes, or generic superlatives.

## GitHub-first posture

Repositories are the source of truth. Keep profile contribution activity, pinned repositories, repository language metadata, commit history, and collaboration surfaces visible in GitHub. The README should point visitors toward those surfaces, never mimic or displace them.
